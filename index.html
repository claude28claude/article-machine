Source. Every article number, official heading and word of official text on this site is extracted from The Constitution of India [As on 1st May, 2024], published by the Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. The amendment history under each article comes from that edition's own footnotes. Checked against the [As on 11th November, 2025] edition, which confirms that no amendment after the 106th has been enacted.

Written for this site. The plain-English explanation of each article, the one-line summary of each amendment, the description of each Schedule and the account of each case. These are summaries, not legal advice, and the official text is printed alongside every one of them so you can check.

The other subjects are written, not extracted. Modern History, Ancient and Medieval History, Indian Economy, Static GK, Biology, Advanced Maths, Geography, the Daily sets and the account of the Constituent Assembly have no single government document behind them, so they are written from the established record. Each data file says at the top which kind of statement it contains and how firm it is — a plan's dates are firm, its achieved growth rate moves by a few tenths between sources, and a current figure carries the date it is true as of. Where the popular answer is wrong — India has no national game, malaria is not bacterial — the page says so rather than repeating it.

Advanced Maths is a formula sheet, not a proof. Every formula is standard competitive-exam mathematics, written in the form an answer sheet wants rather than the form a textbook proves, and checked by working the questions in the practice banks against it. The chapters cover what SSC asks and stop there: there is no calculus, no matrices and no conic section past the plain circle, because those are not on the paper. Rows marked shortcut turn a three-step question into one line; rows marked commonly wrong are the ones candidates reliably get backwards, and the note says which way round it goes. The marks you give yourself in a recall drill stay in this browser and are sent nowhere.

Geography is the arrangement, and the arrangement is what is checked. Heights and lengths that move between sources are given the way the exam gives them and flagged where the disagreement is real — Everest is 8,848.86 m on the 2020 joint Nepal–China survey and older keys still carry 8,848 m. Leading-producer rankings shift year to year, so a row says where the top two are genuinely close rather than asserting one. Disputed ground is named as disputed: K2 stands in the part of the Kashmir region administered by Pakistan, which is why highest peak in India and highest peak lying wholly within India are two different mountains and both are printed.
