What this covers. The three law sections of the Supreme Court Junior Court Assistant 2026 objective test, 35 of its 100 questions: judiciary provisions in the Constitution (15), the Supreme Court Rules, 2013 (10), and the Supreme Court Officers and Servants (Conditions of Service and Conduct) Rules, 1961 (10). English, aptitude, general knowledge and computers are deliberately left out for now.

Extracted. The official text of every article is read from the Article Machine's verbatim extract of The Constitution of India [As on 1st May, 2024], with its plain-English notes and cases, so the two sites say the same thing.

Written, and how firmly. The notes, tables and questions on the two rule books are written for this site. The bare text of the Rules could not be fetched while it was written, so every Order heading and every rule used in a question was checked against at least two independent copies or reports. Anything resting on one source is marked check the bare text, and rules whose headings could not be confirmed are left out rather than guessed. Read the bare text on the Supreme Court's website before the exam: the Rules under Supreme Court Rules, the 1961 Rules under Officers Servant Rules.
